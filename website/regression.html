  • The 1× line

    No difference. A dot on the line means that factor does not change the risk.

  • The dot

    How much the odds change. 2× means double the odds; 0.5× means half.

  • The bar

    The range the true value likely falls in. If it crosses 1×, the effect could be chance.

What this chapter tells us

  • Even in otherwise similar crashes, pedestrians have 9.3 times, motorcyclists 8.6 times and bicyclists 4.9 times the odds of a driver of being seriously hurt or killed.
  • DUI crashes raise the odds 2.5 times, crashes at night 2.1 times, and crashes involving a truck or bus 1.6 times.
  • The place still matters a little: in denser beats the odds are slightly lower.
  • The answer: people outside a car pay the highest price on San Diego streets, and most of all at night and when a driver is drunk.
