What this chapter tells us

  • Of the people hurt in crashes, 14.9% of pedestrians and 14.5% of motorcyclists were seriously hurt or killed.
  • For bicyclists it was 8.2%. For drivers it was 2.2%, and for passengers 2.7%.
  • Unsafe turns are the most common cause police cite, in about 1 in 5 collisions.
  • The most harmful cause is a driver failing to yield to a pedestrian: 18.6% of the people hurt in those crashes were seriously hurt or killed. DUI crashes come next, at 9.0%.
  • These are raw shares. They do not yet account for when or where the crash happened.
