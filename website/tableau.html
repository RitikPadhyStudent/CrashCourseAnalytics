What this chapter tells us

  • Of the people hurt in crashes, 14.9% of pedestrians and 14.5% of motorcyclists were seriously hurt or killed.
  • For bicyclists it was 8.2%. For drivers it was 2.2%, and for passengers 2.7%.
  • These are raw shares. They do not yet account for when the crash happened, what caused it, or where.
