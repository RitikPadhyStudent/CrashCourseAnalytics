Findings

What this chapter tells us

  • Collisions peaked at 10,174 in 2019 and fell about 25% by 2025.
  • Serious and fatal injuries fell only about 5%. Every year, between 4.9% and 5.8% of injured people were seriously hurt or killed.
  • The same beats are hotspots year after year: 21 beats are in the busiest group in at least 6 of the 8 years.
  • Busy is not the same as dangerous: how many crashes a beat has tells you almost nothing about how severe they are.
